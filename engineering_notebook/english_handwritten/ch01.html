1.3 Team Roles & Rules

1. Member Roles

Defining clear roles to make sure training and matches run smoothly.

  • Ye Hengrui: Captain, Builder, Driver (In charge of overall planning and core building)
  • Ye Hengjia: Builder, Driver, Maintenance (In charge of keeping the robot in top shape)
  • Song Shuyue: Driver, Loader, Scout (In charge of on-field coordination and communication)
  • Yin Handi: Loader, Materials Manager (In charge of logistics and pit support)

2. Following the Rules: One Robot Per Team (<R1>)

We strictly follow rule <R1> to ensure fair play:

  1. Only One: A team can only use one robot. We never bring a second assembled robot to a competition.
  2. Inspection: Our robot's design and size must strictly follow the inspection requirements.
  3. Spare Parts: All spare parts are kept as "loose pieces" to avoid any rule-breaking risks.

1.3 Team Roles & Rules (Continued)

3. Equipment Management

Managing our gear properly, down to every last screw and battery.

1. Battery Management (<R12>):

  • Strictly follow the game manual rules for batteries.
  • Label everything: All batteries are numbered. Ye Hengrui and Song Shuyue check the power levels before every training/match.
  • Safety guides are posted in the charging area to ensure timely and safe charging.

2. Firmware & Systems (<R10>):

  • Weekly Checks: Ye Hengrui and Ye Hengjia check VEXos every week to make sure the Brain, Controller, and Motors are up to date.

3. Parts Inventory & Cleanup:

  • Categorized Storage: All parts are sorted into bags and boxes by type (gears, axles, pins, etc.) and labeled.
  • Active Maintenance: We check all robot connections after training. Tools and parts must be put back immediately. We report any needed repairs or new parts as the robot evolves.

1.3 Team Roles & Rules (Continued)

4. Safety Rules (<S1>)

Teacher Wang Xin's "Red Lines" for safety:

  1. Hands Off: Never put your hands near gears or moving parts while the robot is running.
  2. Dry Environment: Never operate in damp areas. Use clean, dry floors outside the competition field.
  3. Waterproof: Keep water bottles and liquids far away from electronics. No "drinking" for the robot!
  4. Careful Carrying: Watch your step when resetting the field or carrying the robot to avoid trips and falls.

1.4 Managing the Team & Solving Problems

1. Respect Rule (<G1>)

Respect is the foundation of teamwork in VEX.

  • Internal: Respect every teammate's ideas. Never laugh at failed attempts. Make everyone feel safe.
  • External: Respect opponents, judges, and volunteers. Win with humility and lose with grace.

2. Core Values: Unity, Diligence, Care, Honor

1. Unity & Collaboration

  • Step In: Always be ready to support your teammates, even outside your main role.
  • Good Communication: Listen carefully and speak up boldly during discussions. Encourage each other and don't blame or complain.

2. Diligence & Study

  • Discipline: Be on time and stay focused during training. Report absences in advance.
  • Never Give Up: Don't quit when things get hard. Ask for help and record every mistake so we can learn from it.

1.4 Managing the Team & Solving Problems (Cont'd)

3. Care for Tools

  • Take Care: Use every tool and our notebook with care. No "brute force"!
  • Clean Field: Keep the training area tidy. Clean up as soon as training ends.

4. Honor & Responsibility

  • Big Picture: Follow tactical plans strictly. Finishing the team's mission is the top priority.
  • Image: Be polite and professional to maintain our team's good reputation.

3. Decision Making & Problem Solving

When we disagree, we follow this process:

1. Tech/Design Disagreements: Let the "data speak." We build prototypes and test them to find the best solution based on results.

2. Tactical/Strategy Disagreements: Use "debate and advice." Everyone shares their thoughts. If we can't agree, we ask our coach.

3. Attendance/Discipline: If someone is late, they must explain why and make up the training time later to keep everyone on track.
